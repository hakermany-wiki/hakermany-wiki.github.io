## Basics
**We can recognize two elementary types of recon:**

## {.tabset}

### Passive
Includes gathering information about target without directly interacting with it. It could be fetching data from public services (e.g. wayback machine, DNS servers, public repos, social media).

### Active
Involves directly interacting with target and possibly leaving traces of our activity (logs). We can probe for open services, versions, hosts and more.
By default this is really noisy and most of defenses recognize this activity as scanning, especially when requests go towards unintended services.
